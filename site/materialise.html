See the same file your engineer sees.

e-Stage 04
e-Stage · automatic supports

Supports generated in seconds, not built by hand.

Automated support generation for resin (SLA, DLP) and metal powder-bed (SLM, DMLS, LPBF) printing.

How it works

  • Lightweight, needle-style supports with minimal contact points
  • Adjustable gusset parameters for stability where it’s needed
  • Open support designs that are easier to clean and de-powder
  • Multi-part stacking to fill the build volume

What it saves

  • Less support material per build
  • Better de-powdering and less surface damage on removal
  • Faster data preparation
  • Shorter post-processing and finishing
Resine-Stage for Resin (SLA, DLP)
Metale-Stage for Metal+ (SLM, DMLS, LPBF)

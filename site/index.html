Data & build preparation

Magics

Materialise’s 3D data and build-preparation platform. It combines CAD and mesh (STL) tools in one place.

  • Repair and edit models with automatic fixing, offsets and lattice structures
  • Import, cut and label parts, slice, and generate build reports
  • Support generation modules: Magics SG for SLA and SG+ for metal
  • Nester module for manual to fully automatic part nesting

Best for Service bureaus and multi-printer, multi-technology print farms.

Ask IME about licensingFull details
Data preparation, subscription

Magics Essentials

The data-preparation edition of Magics, for occasional and frequent users who mainly need clean, printable files.

  • Fix Wizard and ShrinkWrap repair tools
  • Imports and converts most modelling formats, for any 3D printer
  • Basic build preparation
  • Monthly or yearly subscription; Standard and Advanced editions add perpetual and floating licences

Best for Design and engineering teams preparing files for one or two printers.

Ask IME about licensingFull details
Free STL / 3MF viewer

MiniMagics

A free viewer for sharing and checking parts with a 3D printing service, so you see the same file your account manager sees.

  • Rotate, transparent and cross-section views; measure distances, circles and angles
  • Shows dimensions, volume and mesh information
  • Flags file errors such as bad edges, flipped triangles and multiple shells
  • Compresses STL files up to 20× and lets you comment and sign off parts

Best for Anyone sending parts to AMIC for printing or quotation.

Ask IME about licensingFull details
Automatic supports

e-Stage

Automated support-generation software for resin and metal powder-bed printing.

  • Works with resin (SLA, DLP) and metal powder-bed (SLM, DMLS, LPBF) processes
  • Lightweight supports with minimal contact points
  • Cuts data-preparation, support-removal and finishing time
  • Multi-part stacking to fill the build volume

Best for High-volume SLA and DLP production, where support work drives cost.

Ask IME about licensingFull details
Design on mesh

3-matic

Industrial software for refining mesh-level 3D data from CAD, topology optimisation, simulation or 3D scans.

  • Lattice module: lattice, conformal and porous structures
  • Texturing module: textures, patterns and perforations on STL
  • CAD Link module: turn meshes into fully parametric models
  • Post-topology-optimisation smoothing for FEA, plus Python scripting

Best for Design-for-AM engineers, lightweighting and reverse-engineering work.
